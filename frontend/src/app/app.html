How these are worked out

Requirement: HR sets the number of office days for each month ({{ requiredDays() }} for {{ monthName() }}; it changes with holidays). Employees can come in on any days of the month. The requirement is lowered for anyone whose leave leaves fewer eligible days than that. Progress is days recognized in office ÷ required days.

Status: compliant once the required days are reached; at risk if short but enough working days remain this month to still get there; below policy if the requirement can no longer be reached. Office attendance rate is days in office ÷ eligible working days (holidays and approved leave left out).
